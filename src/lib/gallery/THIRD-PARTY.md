# Graphics dependencies

- Three.js 0.163.0 is bundled from npm under its MIT license (`node_modules/three/LICENSE`).
- The zoom blur shader was ported from TroisJS 0.3.0-beta.4. Its MIT notice is retained in `TROIS-LICENSE`.
- Starfield uses eight vivid, contrasting themes curated from the supplied H5's `nice-color-palettes` collection (entries 3, 6, 8, 39, 50, 83, 84 and 95) and its star sprite, hosted locally. A theme is picked randomly on each page load.
- The black hole, accretion disk, stars and lensing shaders were imported from the supplied Colorhole H5.

The Gallery interface does not display demo titles, attribution panels, font imports, or an auto-rotation toggle.
